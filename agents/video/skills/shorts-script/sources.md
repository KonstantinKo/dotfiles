# Evidence for Shorts scripting

Research reviewed 29 September 2026. Use these sources to choose or explain an approach, not
as a checklist of mandatory techniques. Longer Shorts and educational Shorts are separate
questions; neither implies a preferred duration or niche.

## Evidence boundaries

- Platform documentation describes product constraints and stated recommendation signals.
  Check current documentation when a specification, metric, or eligibility rule matters.
- Creator interviews describe personal practice and experience. Public counts, awards, and
  promotional case studies establish noticeable success, not what caused it.
- Examples from long-form or multi-part TikTok establish alternatives worth testing. They do
  not establish that the same execution works in the Shorts feed.
- Recurring formats offer more evidence of repeatability than a single breakout. Neither
  provides a controlled comparison; audience familiarity, topic, timing, and resources matter.
- The suggested transfers below are editorial synthesis unless explicitly attributed.

## Narrative structure and creator voice

The [compact narrative playbook](references/compact-narrative.md) turns this pathway into a
usable writing and review procedure. Its numerical defaults are drafting heuristics, not
algorithm thresholds; do not attribute every composition or editing target to a creator.

- **Jenny Hoyos / Creator Science (2023):** hook, foreshadow, narrative, satisfying surprise;
  compact language and writing toward an ending. Retain this as an option for surprise stories.
  Her roughly 34-second target and platform preferences describe her audience and that period,
  before three-minute YouTube Shorts. [Original interview](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/jenny-hoyos/)
- **Colin & Samir / South Park story principle:** causal connections help narrative momentum.
  Apply “but/therefore” to stories; it does not establish that tutorials or chronological
  transformations must fail. [Discussion](https://www.musicbed.com/articles/filmmaking/youtube/the-biggest-mistake-filmmakers-make-with-colin-and-samir/)
- **Jordan Howlett / YouTube (2025):** personal experience, authenticity, and original ideas
  beyond trends. A separate 2024 profile describes his minimalist presentation and recognizable
  ritual, with over 29 million cross-platform followers. Recognition can support attention;
  new creators have not yet earned the same familiarity.
  [Creator advice](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/five-tips-to-master-shorts/),
  [profile](https://sandiegomagazine.com/features/jordan-howlett-tik-tok-influencer/)

## Educational content, independent of length

- **Cleo Abram and producer Justin Poore (2025):** write around visual explanations, read
  scripts aloud, and construct complete Shorts while reusing research and graphics. Abram also
  describes rerecording vertical stories from larger investigations. Reuse the reporting;
  ensure each Short stands alone.
  [Poore interview, around 7–9 and 20–24 minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l8bBdWYCh1Q&t=420s),
  [Abram interview, around 17–23 minutes](https://www.levels.com/podcasts/195-video-content-creation-and-maintaining-journalistic-integrity-cleo-abram-ben-grynol)
- **Two verified Abram examples:** [Why Do All The Planes Fly This Line?](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8zegi6-ZyiM)
  runs 51 seconds; [New Alien Host Planet Just Dropped](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Wrps-lZQME8)
  runs 59 seconds. Metadata and transcripts were inspected; public listings showed roughly
  4.1 million and 6.4 million views at research time. Both include subscribe invitations.
  These establish successful examples beyond 34 seconds and with CTAs, not an optimal length
  or evidence that CTAs improved performance. No shot-by-shot claims follow from transcripts.
- **Dr Julie Smith (2026):** develops and cuts scripts, translates abstract ideas into visual
  treatments with her husband, and practices spoken delivery. Her observations about which
  topics attract attention are useful hypotheses, not a mandate to sensationalize problems.
  [Interview, process at 24:11, hooks at 39:19, delivery at 51:37](https://adamstott.com/dr-julie-smith-the-reason-youre-not-doing-the-thing-you-know-you-should/)
- **Hank Green (2024):** discusses bringing viewers through investigations and making
  corrections engaging. Discovery supplies progression without invented jeopardy. The examples
  include long-form work, not proof of a three-minute Shorts formula.
  [WNYC interview](https://www.wnycstudios.org/podcasts/otm/articles/how-hank-green-makes-the-truth-go-viral-plus-the-escape-fantasies-of-the-uber-rich)
- **Will Flanary / Dr. Glaucomflecken (2025):** describes developing comedy that explains
  complex healthcare issues and learning from specialists before teaching. IHI reported an
  audience above five million. Stories and recognizable conflicts can introduce complexity;
  audience-specific context need not be erased to satisfy a reading-level rule.
  [Interview](https://www.ihi.org/library/blog/physician-will-flanary-comedy-and-connection-health-care)
- **Alec Watson / Technology Connections (2022):** the interview hosts describe calm, humorous
  explanations of ordinary objects and report 1.7 million subscribers. An overlooked question
  can make a familiar subject interesting. This inference comes from the hosts' description,
  not a verified transcript of Watson prescribing a scripting method.
  [Interview page](https://www.chadandsteve.com/episode/alec-watson-is-making-technology-connections-ep-79/)

## Longer formats, independent of niche

- **Charles Gross, Ana / Salty Cocina, Andrew / Black Brilliance (2021):** direct testimony in
  TikTok's selected creator feature describes using additional time for storytelling, recipe
  explanation, and important scenes. Self-reported performance supports testing warranted
  length, not padding to a platform maximum.
  [TikTok interviews](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/longer-video-creators?lang=en)
- **Dan Toomey (2022):** describes 60–90-second Instagram/TikTok stories, experimentation,
  and repeating formats that connect. Conceptual comedy can omit familiar context while
  retaining a complete premise. [Interview](https://teuxdeux.com/blog/dan-toomey-interview)
- **Max Klymenko / Career Ladder:** an original post's transcript sets a two-minute job-guessing
  challenge and progresses through clues and revised guesses. The stated clock is not a
  measured upload duration. TikTok named him UK/Ireland Creator of the Year in 2025. A game can
  supply structure without scripting the guest's answers.
  [Original post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/maxoklymenko_business-careers-careeradvice-activity-7308280084858060800-3cO2),
  [award](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-winners-of-the-tiktok-awards-uk-ireland-2025-with-headline-partner-sky?lang=en-GB)
- **Reesa Teesa (2024):** a 50-part story totaling roughly eight hours broke out on TikTok;
  reported series views exceeded 400 million. Those are cumulative views, not completed series
  or unique viewers. Her advice emphasized telling the story fully. Knowing the outcome need
  not remove interest in how it happened. An exceptional series is weak evidence of a reusable
  format and does not imply those episode lengths fit YouTube Shorts.
  [GMA interview](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Living/tiktok-star-reesa-teesa-opens-marriage-awry-gma/story?id=107732860),
  [CultureCon reporting](https://www.ajc.com/things-to-do/arts-culture/reesa-teesa-speaks-on-tiktok-journey-next-steps-at-clark-atlantas-culturecon/L7FFLXW6ZVFIVPMBQH6HBGREWM/)

## Presentation and process counterexamples

- **John Plant / Primitive Technology (2019):** says friends advised narration; he retained
  silence and added optional explanatory subtitles. Outside reported 9.8 million subscribers.
  Visible construction can carry progression and invite questions without spoken narration.
  This is long-form evidence, and it depends on observable changes.
  [Interview](https://www.outsideonline.com/culture/books-media/john-plant-primitive-technology-youtube-channel?scope=anon)
- **Nara Smith (2024):** the profile describes quiet delivery alongside close process shots and
  quick cuts, with 14.9 million combined TikTok/Instagram followers. Calm voice does not imply
  little visual activity or little production effort.
  [Interview/profile](https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/cover-features/nara-smith)
- **Khaby Lame:** identifies silence as central to accessible visual comedy. His simple
  solutions are followed by reactions that help complete the joke. TikTok recorded 100 million
  followers in 2021. The practical answer may appear before the emotional payoff is finished.
  [Interview](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/khaby-lame-interview/),
  [milestone](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/congratulations-khaby-lame-for-100-m-followers-bringing-joy-to-our-global-community?lang=en)
- **B. Dylan Hollis:** describes discovering an audience for testing unusual vintage recipes.
  A 2022 profile reported nine million TikTok followers. Procedural steps can hold interest when
  they advance a meaningful test and provide reactions along the way.
  [Interview](https://newsisout.com/2023/08/tiktok-star-b-dylan-hollis-recipe-for-international-success/18730/),
  [profile](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2022/dec/07/tiktoks-b-dylan-hollis-gives-wacky-vintage-cookboo/)
- **Zach King:** describes preparation and detailed editing, including reshoots. Guinness
  recorded 2.2 billion views for his broomstick illusion in March 2022. Polished craft can fit
  a social feed; his production resources are not a default budget for other creators.
  [Interview transcript](https://www.wired.com/video/watch/tech-support-zach-king-answers-tiktok-questions-from-twitter),
  [historical record](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/689105-most-viewed-video-on-tiktok)

## Platforms and measurement

- **YouTube documentation:** eligible square/vertical Shorts can run up to three minutes.
  Recommendation signals include choosing to watch, average duration, percentage viewed, and
  satisfaction; audience interests and competition also matter. These do not prescribe a
  single script genre or optimum runtime.
  [Duration/eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en),
  [Shorts discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en),
  [views versus engaged views](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/)
- **Paddy Galloway's 2023 observational study:** useful historical evidence about opening
  appeal and duration/retention relationships. Reported ranges are not universal thresholds;
  the study predates three-minute Shorts. Do not turn associations into a causal diagnosis or
  use them to dismiss platform-documented satisfaction signals.
  [Original thread](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898356419981315),
  [creator's summary](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paddy-galloway-459b8913a_we-studied-33-billion-views-to-decode-the-activity-7053697597592498176-PAkf)
- **TikTok:** Search Insights documents question-led discovery; the 2024 Creator Rewards
  announcement describes incentives for original videos over a minute. Reward eligibility is
  not evidence that extending a script improves distribution.
  [Search Insights](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights?lang=en),
  [Rewards announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)
- **Instagram:** Meta discusses Reels and messaging; three-minute support was reported in
  2025. This supports testing shareable, complete videos rather than assuming every Reel must
  be extremely short. These sources do not establish current ranking weights or every upload
  limit; verify specifications when necessary.
  [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/in-india-instagram-debuts-a-reels-first-experience-for-its-mobile-app/amp/),
  [support reporting](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/27/instagram-now-lets-you-speed-up-videos-just-like-on-tiktok/)
- **Kevin Espiritu / Epic Gardening (2021):** describes adapting material to viewing context
  and sometimes recording from bullet points. A common core plus appropriate presentation is
  a useful starting hypothesis; identical performance across platforms is not established.
  [Interview](https://www.smartpassiveincome.com/podcasts/niche-that-exploded-during-pandemic/)

Use current analytics definitions and channel-specific comparisons. A retention dip can have
several causes; high repeat viewing can reflect pleasure, detail, or confusion. Comments can
reveal understanding or misconceptions. The diagnostic suggestions in SKILL.md are hypotheses
for inspection and testing, not claims made verbatim by any one source.
