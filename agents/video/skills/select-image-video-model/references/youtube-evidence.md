# Creator YouTube tests: supplemental image and video evidence

Research date: 2026-10-01. Creator-run tests provide useful prompts, task examples, and possible failure patterns. Subtitle retrieval for two videos returned HTTP 429, including a request after the required cooldown. No transcript or video frame was obtained. The notes distinguish accessible metadata and companion-page findings from claims that need original-video verification.

## Candidate video evidence

### 1. Grégory Beyrouti — Seedance 2.5 vs MiniMax H3 vs FLUX 3

**Video:** [Quelle IA vidéo choisir ? Seedance 2.5 vs MiniMax H3 vs Flux 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMNxMeKf-2I) · published 2026-08-18 · creator identified as Grégory Beyrouti. Companion page: [test and notes](https://gregory-beyrouti.com/videos/seedance-25-vs-minimax-h3-vs-flux-3/).

The companion page describes four same-prompt blind tests, one generation per model, no retries: a 15-second food-truck take, four-reference product sequence, basketball contact/physics, and French dialogue/lip-sync. It names Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3/Hailuo 3, and FLUX 3 Video. The visible food-truck prompt follows a cook through dusk into neon night with a camera arc and scene sound; three test prompts are gated. The page favors 2.5 for duration/coherence, H3 for value, Flux for a natural look, and says contact remains difficult. Pollo AI affiliate link is explicit. Transcript unavailable; low–moderate confidence.

### 2. Pouya Eti — Seedance 2.5 vs MiniMax H3 on six ads

**Video:** [Seedance 2.5 vs Minimax H3: I Tested Both on 6 Real Ads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zT8zGtRGO9E) · published 2026-08-19 · Pouya Eti. Mirror/source description: [Zolotube page](https://zolotube.com/watch?v=zT8zGtRGO9E).

The description reports six 720p ad tests, each starting from a real source image, with Gemini Omni as a third model in most: energy drink, pizza, 3D action/physics, spicy chips, ice cream, and a walking 3D character. It says Seedance 2.5 won four, H3 the ice-cream scene, and Omni the chips scene, then advises selecting shots per scene. Omni version, prompts, retries, and route are unspecified. Links promote Rangy AI and Eti’s plugin/courses; explicit sponsorship is not stated. Transcript unavailable; results unverified; low–moderate confidence.

It does not specify Grok version, resolution, audio mode, route, retries, or sponsor status in the visible metadata. Transcript unavailable; do not reuse ranking claims from third-party digests as observed results. **Evidence:** useful candidate coverage of the Kling/Veo/Grok families, but methods are incomplete; low confidence until transcript/outputs are inspected.

The other two models, prompt/settings, route beyond fal sandbox, and sponsorship/disclosures are not established in the visible notes. ReelStack says it did not test the claims itself. Transcript request returned 429. **Evidence:** potentially useful H3 Max test lead; not usable as a result until checked against the original transcript/visible output; low confidence.

## What changes in the decision guide

No model recommendation should change on these sources alone because no transcript or generated output was inspectable. Pouya Eti’s description reports six source-image ad tasks and says Seedance 2.5, H3, and Gemini Omni each win different scenes. Gregory’s separate four-round blind comparison tests Seedance 2.5, H3, and FLUX 3 across long-take continuity, product references, contact physics, and French dialogue. These are hypotheses to validate, not new general rules. Marin Method and YaroFlasher are dated leads for Kling/Veo/Grok; routes and several settings remain unknown.
