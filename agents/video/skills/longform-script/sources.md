# Sources: long-form YouTube scripts

## Packaging and ideas
- Paddy Galloway: idea sets the ceiling; top creators spend ~30% of time on ideation and
  packaging vs ~5% for small creators; title + thumbnail as one; glance test.
  - https://www.colinandsamir.com/resources/the-new-rules-of-youtube-from-paddy-galloway
  - https://podcast.creatorscience.com/paddy-galloway-2/
  - https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1811083499044044951
- Veritasium, "Clickbait is Unreasonably Effective" (2021): product vs packaging; legitbait
  vs clicktraps; the Magnus effect title example; titles/thumbnails changed after publishing.
  - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S2xHZPH5Sng
  - https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20210830-clickbait-effective/
- MrBeast production memo ("How to Succeed in MrBeast Production", leaked 2024): first
  minute most important; CTR / AVD / AVP; "50 Hours In Ketchup"; re-engagement around
  minute 3; "the best YOUTUBE videos possible". Internal document for entertainment content;
  use only the transferable parts.
  - https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/
  - https://www.creatorhandbook.net/leaked-document-allegedly-reveals-mrbeasts-secrets-to-youtube-success-the-key-takeaways/

## Learning and explanation
- Derek Muller (Veritasium) PhD research: misconception-first videos felt more confusing
  but nearly doubled test scores vs clear expository videos.
  - https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/how-youtube-star-derek-muller-of-veritasium-is-challenging-scientific/
  - https://www.veritasium.com/publications
- Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown): concrete before abstract; examples before frameworks; key
  question first; discovery; narrative; topic over production; get a good mic; niche is fine.
  - https://www.antoinebuteau.com/lessons-from-grant-sanderson/
  - https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/grant-sanderson
  - https://blog.dropbox.com/topics/work-culture/grant-sanderson-channels-his-passion-for-math-into-marvelously-i

## Craft habits
- Tom Scott: clarity over over-explaining; deep research; public corrections; weekly for
  ten years as escalating improvement.
  - https://simonwillison.net/2024/Jan/2/escalating-streaks/
  - https://async.com/blog/tom-scott-youtube/

## Evidence notes
- The 30% / 5% time split and the minute-3 re-engagement are practitioner claims, not studies.
- Muller's finding comes from a controlled study with first-year physics students; it is the
  strongest evidence in this skill.
